Statistical framework

Trait-centered multi-year analysis

Genotype performance is evaluated separately for each trait. Genotype × year variance components are estimated using a balanced genotype × year × replicate ANOVA framework when supported by the dataset. Broad-sense heritability is calculated from genotype, genotype × year, and residual variance components.

The genotype × year visualization uses accession rank instead of accession names on the axis to prevent label overlap in datasets containing hundreds of accessions. Accession identifiers remain available through interactive hover information.

BLUP-style values use year-level genotype means with empirical shrinkage. For unbalanced designs, spatial models, or complex random-effect structures, formal REML should be used for publication-grade variance-component estimation.
