SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

Interpretation of Network Relationships

Network connectivity describes the relationships displayed by the selected network model. A connection by itself does not establish a physical interaction, regulatory relationship, causal relationship, or biological function.

Biological interpretation should be based on the underlying expression, interaction, regulatory, or other validated evidence used to construct the network.
