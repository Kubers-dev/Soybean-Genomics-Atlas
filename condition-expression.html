Dataset

SCN_W82 soybean root expression dataset. Expression values are summarized at the condition level from three biological replicates per condition. The expression dataset contains the condition-level TPM, log₂(TPM + 1), and gene-wise Z-score values. Gene classification, GO identifiers, transcription-factor annotation, and differential-expression statistics are loaded from the corresponding SCN_W82 website metadata.
